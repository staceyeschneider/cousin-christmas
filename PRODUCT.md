# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JavaScript (countdown, snowfall, lightbox). Keep it static: no framework, no build step. The 2022-2025 year pages share `assets/css/style.css`; the 2026 page (`index.html`) uses its own stylesheet so the archives are never restyled by accident.

## Users

Mostly the cousins: kids and teens, roughly 12 to 17. They open the site on their phones to check the plan for a family Christmas weekend: when it starts, where the cabin is, what to wear, what there is to do, and who is coming. Parents and grandparents visit too, mainly for the address and the Sunday plan.

## Product Purpose

The hub for Cousin Christmas 2026 at Harper's Hideaway, 46 Pelican Dr, Ellijay, GA 30540, inside the Coosawattee River Resort, December 18-21, 2026. The cousins' sleepover is Saturday, December 19; the family potluck Christmas and gift exchange is Sunday, December 20. Success: every cousin knows when and where to show up, shows up in Christmas PJs, and is excited to go.

## Positioning

A family tradition with its own cast, logo, history, and running jokes (seven cousins, yearly themes since 2022, a "Schneider Family Corp." voice). This year it moves from the Carolyn house to a mountain cabin with a game room.

## Operating Context

- Read mostly on phones, often in a group chat link, in the weeks before the weekend.
- Countdown targets Saturday, December 19, 2026, 3 PM Eastern.
- Each year gets an archive page (`/2022` to `/2025`); the 2026 page links to them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: hero with countdown, The Situation letter, The Plan (Saturday and Sunday schedules), dress code, The Cabin (address, map embed, directions and Airbnb links), Free Play (Game Room, VR, Jackbox lists), The Cousins cast, The Menu, last year's gallery with photo and video lightbox, The Archives, footer.
- Open placeholders, kept as-is until the family confirms: the 3 PM Saturday start time, and the potluck "Sign-ups coming soon."

## Brand Commitments

Binding (confirmed by Stacey):
- The cousin photos and names: William, Johnny, Lindsey, Addy, Kaylee, Luke, James.
- The countdown.
- Last year's photo and video gallery with its lightbox.
- Links to past years 2022-2025.

## Product Principles

1. The plan is the point: date, place, dress code, and start time are findable in seconds on a phone.
2. It belongs to the cousins: fun and a little ridiculous, never a stuffy lodge brochure.
3. Keep the tradition visible: cast, logo, and archives carry over year to year.
4. Don't invent facts; placeholders stay labeled until the family confirms them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast on phones in any light, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support for the snowfall and other animation.
