# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JavaScript (countdown, lightbox). Keep it static: no framework, no build step. The 2022-2025 year pages share `assets/css/style.css`; the 2026 page (`index.html`) uses its own stylesheet, `assets/css/2026.css`, so the archives are never restyled by accident.

## Users

Mostly the cousins: kids and teens, roughly 12 to 17. They open the site on their phones to check the plan: when it starts, where the house is, what to wear, what there is to do, and who is coming. Parents and grandparents visit too, mainly for the address and the Christmas Eve potluck time.

## Product Purpose

The hub for Cousin Christmas 2026, themed **Cousins Home Alone**, at Nana & Papa Jack's house, 508 Townsend Street, Grayson, GA (no ZIP on the page; the family has not confirmed one). Nana and Papa Jack are "out of town" (they clear out for the party), so the cousins have the house to themselves.

- Wednesday, December 23 (the regular Cousin Christmas date): the cousins' slumber party at the Grayson house, starting at noon. Jackbox and games; takeout is the dinner plan; the cousins sleep over that night.
- Thursday, December 24 (Christmas Eve): at 2 PM the whole family comes for a potluck, followed by the gift exchange.

Success: every cousin knows when and where to show up, shows up in Christmas PJs, and is excited to go.

## Positioning

A family tradition with its own cast, logo, history, and running jokes (seven cousins, yearly themes since 2022, a "Schneider Family Corp." voice). The Carolyn house is sold; this year's theme is an affectionate homage to 90s family holiday movies: the grandparents leave, the kids have the house. Homage only: no movie stills, posters, logos or the film's title lettering, and every nod stays clean and non-violent (no traps that hurt anyone).

## Operating Context

- Read mostly on phones, often from a group-chat link, in the weeks before the party.
- Countdown targets Wednesday, December 23, 2026, noon Eastern (`2026-12-23T12:00:00-05:00`), the start of the slumber party.
- Each year gets an archive page (`/2022` to `/2025`); the 2026 page links to them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: poster hero (title, tagline, VCR countdown, the house at night, billing block), The Setup, The Plan (Wednesday and Thursday, blueprint), Christmas PJ dress code, Filming Location (address, map embed, directions link, unofficial house rules), Now Playing (Jackbox), Starring (cast), The Menu (ticket: Wednesday takeout, Thursday potluck), last year's gallery with photo and video lightbox, The Prequels (archive links), footer.
- Open placeholders, kept as-is until the family confirms: the potluck "Sign-ups coming soon." The "Carolyn house is sold" line stays for now.
- Games: Jackbox only. No VR this year. Do not list equipment the house may not have (ping pong, darts, arcade games, and so on).

## Brand Commitments

Binding (confirmed by Stacey):
- The cousin photos and names: William, Johnny, Lindsey, Addy, Kaylee, Luke, James.
- The countdown.
- Last year's photo and video gallery with its lightbox.
- Links to past years 2022-2025.
- The Cousin Christmas logo (`assets/images/CC-Logo-Transparent.png`, the reindeer in sunglasses), readable in the nav on its cream plaque.
- The Christmas PJ dress code.
- Voice: playful, dry, teasing family humor with gentle 90s-movie nods ("Keep the change, ya filthy animals"). Proofread for repeated phrases; only names, dates, the address and archive titles may repeat.

## Product Principles

1. The plan is the point: dates, place, dress code, the noon slumber-party start and the 2 PM Christmas Eve potluck are findable in seconds on a phone.
2. It belongs to the cousins: fun and a little ridiculous, never a formal invitation.
3. Keep the tradition visible: cast, logo, and archives carry over year to year.
4. Don't invent facts; placeholders stay labeled until the family confirms them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast on phones in any light, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support for the snowfall, window lights, VCR blink and hover motion.
