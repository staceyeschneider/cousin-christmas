The Situation

Yes, it's October. The Carolyn house is sold, which means this is the final Cousin Christmas under this roof — so Christmas is coming early, and it's bringing a moving truck.

You asked for less schedule and more hanging out. Done. No teams. No points. No trophy. Just cousins, Christmas PJs, trees and a frankly irresponsible amount of takeout.

Dress code

It's a Christmas PJ Party

Christmas pajamas are the uniform from the moment you walk in until the parents show up Sunday. Reindeer, plaid, candy canes, a onesie shaped like a gingerbread man — the louder the better.

Christmas PJs: required Matching sets: bonus Slippers: strongly advised
The main event

Take a Tree Home

This year, instead of a heart, I'm giving you a tree.

Every cousin gets one. Pick your ornaments from the stash, decorate it however you want, and it goes home with you. I'm downsizing. You're upsizing. Everybody wins.

  • Your tree, your style. No judges this year.
  • Every ornament you hang leaves with you.
  • No returns. No exchanges. Tinsel is non-refundable.
  • Make sure your ride home has room for a tree. That one's on you.
