The Situation

A word from the management

The Carolyn house is sold, so Cousin Christmas is going on the road. This year we're taking over Harper's Hideaway, a cabin in the North Georgia mountains with a game room that frankly should not be legal.

You asked for less schedule and more hanging out. Done. No teams. No points. No trophy. Just cousins, Christmas PJs, ping pong, Skee-Ball and an irresponsible amount of takeout.
